HUNARSAAZ PORTAL — WORKSHOP REGISTRATION
========================================

Goes to   /home2/lqonfyud/hunarsaaz-portal   (NOT public_html)


YOUR DATABASE IS NOT TOUCHED
----------------------------

There is deliberately NO data folder and NO .env file in this zip, so
neither can be overwritten. You keep your admin account, instructors,
photographs, students, enrolments and payment records.

This release ADDS tables to your database when it starts, for workshops
and registrations, and adds two columns to one of Payload's own tables.
It creates them only if they are missing and changes nothing already
there.


WHAT IS NEW
-----------

A registration page for THRIVE 360:

    https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/workshops/thrive-360

Anyone can open it. No account, no login. They fill in their name, email
and phone, choose Professional (Rs 2,000) or Student (Rs 1,000), say how
many places, and press register.

They immediately get a payment reference and your bank details on screen
and by email. The same payment appears in Payments in the portal, next to
your donations, so confirming it is the job you already know.

They can also ATTACH THEIR PAYMENT RECEIPT — a photo of the transfer
slip, a screenshot from their banking app, or the bank's PDF. There is an
upload box on the screen straight after registering, and a link in their
confirmation email that brings them back to it later, which is what most
people will use because they pay after registering.

When a receipt arrives you get an email about it. The receipt itself is
on the registration in /admin, under Workshop Registrations. Open it,
check it against the bank, then confirm the payment in Payments — which
now marks their place confirmed as well, so it is not two jobs.

Receipts are NOT public. They are photographs of bank transfers, so they
are stored outside the public folder and only you and your instructors
can open them.

You can see every registration in /admin > Workshops > Workshop
Registrations: name, email, phone, organisation, professional or student,
how many places, and whether they have paid.

The workshop itself is in /admin > Workshops > Workshops. Change the
price, the time, the outcomes, anything — it is an ordinary record. The
Zoom link goes in the "Join URL" field, which is NEVER shown on the
public page; it is for emailing to people who have paid.

IMPORTANT — THIS ALSO FIXES THE LIVE LECTURES RELEASE
-----------------------------------------------------

The live-lectures bundle sent earlier has a fault that only shows on a
database older than that feature: scheduling a class would save the class
and then throw an error. This release fixes it. If you installed the
live-lectures bundle, install this one too.


STEP 1 — BACK UP THE DATABASE
-----------------------------
cPanel > File Manager > /home2/lqonfyud/hunarsaaz-portal > data

Right-click hunarsaaz.db > Download. Keep it safe.


STEP 2 — STOP THE APP
---------------------
cPanel > Setup Node.js App > your application > Stop


STEP 3 — DELETE ONLY THESE FIVE
-------------------------------
In /home2/lqonfyud/hunarsaaz-portal, delete:

    .next
    node_modules
    src
    server.js
    package.json

DO NOT DELETE:

    data              your database
    .env              your settings and password key
    public            your uploaded photographs
    private-uploads   student work

File Manager > Settings > Show Hidden Files to see .next and .env.


STEP 4 — UPLOAD AND EXTRACT
---------------------------
Three zips, because the whole thing is too big to download in one piece:

    portal-part1-app.zip
    portal-part2-next.zip
    portal-part3-rest.zip

Upload all three, then extract each one in turn. Order does not matter,
but all three must be extracted or the app will not start. Delete the
zips afterwards.


STEP 5 — START IT
-----------------
Setup Node.js App > Start

DO NOT click "Run NPM Install".


STEP 6 — CHECK
--------------
Open, with Ctrl+Shift+R:

    https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/workshops/thrive-360

You should see the workshop with Ms. Asma Ahmed, the fees, "Who can
attend", and the registration form on the right.

Register yourself with a real email address as a test. You should get a
reference on screen and an email.

Then attach any photo as a test receipt, using the box on the same
screen. You should get a second email telling you a receipt arrived.

Look in /admin > Workshop Registrations: you will see yourself, with the
receipt attached, and the payment in Payments.

Delete your test registration and test receipt afterwards from /admin.


BEFORE YOU SHARE THE FLYER
--------------------------

Scan the QR code with your own phone first and check it opens the
registration page. If the page is not live yet, the code will open
nothing and anyone who scans it will think the workshop is not real.


A NOTE ON THE private-uploads FOLDER
------------------------------------

Receipts are written to private-uploads/receipts inside the portal
folder, alongside the student work already in private-uploads. As with
everything else there, do not delete that folder when you upgrade — the
instructions above are careful to leave it alone.


ONE THING TO SET
----------------

The Zoom link. Open /admin > Workshops > THRIVE 360 and put it in the
"Join URL" field under "When and where". It is not shown publicly — it
is there so you have it in one place when you email the people who have
paid.
