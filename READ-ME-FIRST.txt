HUNARSAAZ PORTAL — FEES REMOVED
===============================

Goes to   /home2/lqonfyud/hunarsaaz-portal   (NOT public_html)


YOUR DATABASE IS NOT TOUCHED
----------------------------

There is deliberately NO data folder and NO .env file in this zip, so
neither can be overwritten.

You keep:

    your admin account
    your instructors and their photographs
    your students, enrolments and payment records
    your settings in .env

Do NOT delete the data, .env, public/uploads or private-uploads folders.


WHAT CHANGED
------------

Course fees no longer appear anywhere in the portal, and enrolling never
asks anyone to pay.

    the "Fee" line on a course page is gone
    the price on the catalogue cards is gone
    "Free to enrol" wording is gone as well, because you are invoicing
      students separately and the two would contradict each other
    a student who enrols is admitted straight away
    nobody is sent to a bank-transfer page any more
    anyone still stuck on "awaiting payment" from before is let in

Donations are untouched and still work exactly as they did. Your old
payment records are all still there in the finance page.

Nothing is deleted from the database. The price field is still on each
course in /admin, it is simply not shown to students and not charged. If
you ever want fees back, tell me and it is one line to switch on.


STEP 1 — BACK UP THE DATABASE ANYWAY
------------------------------------
cPanel > File Manager > /home2/lqonfyud/hunarsaaz-portal > data

Right-click hunarsaaz.db > Download. One file. It is how you get back if
anything goes wrong.


STEP 2 — STOP THE APP
---------------------
cPanel > Setup Node.js App > your application > Stop


STEP 3 — DELETE ONLY THESE FIVE
-------------------------------
In /home2/lqonfyud/hunarsaaz-portal, delete:

    .next
    node_modules
    src
    server.js
    package.json

DO NOT DELETE:

    data              your database
    .env              your settings and password key
    public            your uploaded photographs are in public/uploads
    private-uploads   student work

Deleting "public" would take every instructor photograph with it. Leave
it alone — the new files merge into it.

Turn on File Manager > Settings > Show Hidden Files to see .next and .env.


STEP 4 — UPLOAD AND EXTRACT
---------------------------
Upload hunarsaaz-portal-nofee.zip into hunarsaaz-portal.
Right-click it > Extract. Let it overwrite when asked.
Then delete the zip.

Afterwards these should all sit directly in hunarsaaz-portal:

    server.js   package.json   READ-ME-FIRST.txt   .env
    .next   node_modules   public   data   private-uploads   src

"data" and ".env" are still there — they are yours, from before.


STEP 5 — START IT
-----------------
Setup Node.js App > Start

DO NOT click "Run NPM Install".


STEP 6 — CHECK
--------------
Open these and press Ctrl+Shift+R on each:

    https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/learn/courses
      no price on any card

    https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/learn/courses/graphic-design
      the box on the right says "Enrol in this course", with no Fee line

Sign in as a student and press Enrol now. You should land straight back
on the course page saying "You are enrolled" — no payment page.


ONE THING TO CHECK IN .env
--------------------------

Open .env in the File Manager editor and make sure it says

    SITE_URL=https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk

If that address does not match the address people actually use, enrolling
silently fails and sends the student to the login page instead. Leave
everything else in the file alone, especially PAYLOAD_SECRET — changing
it logs everybody out.
