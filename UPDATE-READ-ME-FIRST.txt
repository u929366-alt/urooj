HUNARSAAZ PORTAL — UPDATE
Workshop registration now waits for you to check the payment
=============================================================

WHAT THIS CHANGES

  Before: someone filled in the form and was registered. Nobody checked
          whether they had actually paid.

  Now:    they pay first, attach a photo of the transfer slip, and the
          form will not send without it. They get an email that says
          "we have your registration" — NOT a confirmation.

          You then open a new page, look at the receipt next to the
          amount and the reference, and press "Payment verified —
          approve". ONLY THAT sends them the confirmation email with
          the joining link. Nothing else sends it.

          If the payment is wrong, press "Something's wrong", type a
          line explaining why, and they get that by email instead.

  The new page is at:  https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/learn/workshops
  It is also in the portal menu as "Workshops" (admins and teachers
  only — students never see it).

  The confirmation email is Ms. Asma Ahmed's letter, word for word.
  It is already in the system — you do not have to paste it in. If you
  ever want to change it:

      /admin -> Workshops -> THRIVE 360 -> "Confirmation email"

  It is in two boxes, one for the paragraphs above the Workshop
  Details table and one for the paragraphs below it, ending with the
  sign-off. The greeting ("Dear <their name>,"), the line confirming
  their payment, and the Details table are added around it
  automatically, so the date, time and speaker can never drift out of
  step with the workshop record.


WHAT YOU ARE UPLOADING

  This is a SMALL update. The big node_modules folder has NOT changed,
  so you do not touch it at all. Only five things go up:

      .next          (folder)
      public         (folder)
      src            (folder)
      server.js      (file)
      package.json   (file)

  Total about 40 MB extracted. Last time it was 190 MB.


STEP BY STEP
============

STEP 1 — Unzip it on your computer

  Right-click  portal-update.zip  ->  "Extract All..."  ->  Extract.
  You now have a folder called  portal-update  containing the five
  things listed above.


STEP 2 — Open FileZilla and connect

  Same connection you used last time. On the RIGHT-hand side (the
  server), go into the folder:

      /hunarsaaz-portal


STEP 3 — Put the old .next safely to one side (DO NOT DELETE IT)

  On the right-hand side, find the folder called  .next
  Right-click it  ->  Rename  ->  type:   .next-old
  Press Enter.

  This is your way back if anything goes wrong. Do not skip it.

  (If FileZilla is not showing .next, click Server menu ->
   "Force showing hidden files".)


STEP 4 — Upload the five things

  On the LEFT-hand side of FileZilla, go into your  portal-update
  folder.

  Select all five:  .next, public, src, server.js, package.json
  Drag them to the RIGHT-hand side, into /hunarsaaz-portal

  If it asks "file already exists", choose:
      Action: Overwrite
      and tick "Always use this action"  then OK.

  Wait for the transfer queue at the bottom to go completely empty.
  It should take a few minutes. DO NOT restart anything until it is
  empty and shows no failed transfers.

  If any file says "failed" in the bottom window, right-click it and
  choose "Reset and requeue" until it goes through.


STEP 5 — Restart the portal

  Go to cPanel  ->  Setup Node.js App  ->  find the portal app  ->
  press RESTART.

  Wait about 30 seconds.


STEP 6 — Check it worked

  Open these two pages:

    https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/learn          (should load as normal)
    https://hunarsaaz.pk/workshops/thrive-360 (the registration form)

  On the registration form you should now see three numbered steps:
    Step 1 — pay
    Step 2 — your details
    Step 3 — attach your receipt

  Then log in at https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/learn/login and open
    https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/learn/workshops
  It should say "Workshop registrations".

  IF ALL THREE WORK  ->  go to Step 7.
  IF SOMETHING IS BROKEN  ->  go to "IF IT GOES WRONG" below.


STEP 7 — Tidy up

  Back in FileZilla, right-click  .next-old  ->  Delete.
  That is it. You are done.


STEP 8 — On or before 10 November

  The letter says the platform will be shared by email on 10 November,
  so until then the confirmation email prints exactly that under
  Venue/Platform. Nothing to do now.

  When you have the Zoom link:

    Go to  https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/admin
    ->  Workshops  ->  THRIVE 360  ->  "Join URL"
    ->  paste the Zoom link  ->  Save.

  From then on every confirmation carries the link and a "Join on
  Zoom" button, and the Venue/Platform line changes by itself. People
  confirmed BEFORE you set it will not have it, so they still need the
  link sent round on the 10th.

  (The line shown until then is editable too: same page, "Venue note".)


IF IT GOES WRONG
================

  Nothing is lost. Put the old version back:

    1. In FileZilla, delete the new  .next  folder.
    2. Rename  .next-old  back to  .next
    3. cPanel -> Setup Node.js App -> RESTART.

  The portal is exactly as it was. Then tell me what you saw and I
  will fix it.


NOTES
=====

  * Your database is not touched. Every student, course and payment
    stays exactly where it is. The two new bits of information the
    portal needs (who checked a payment, and when) are added by the
    portal itself the first time it starts up.

  * Nobody who already registered is affected. Existing registrations
    simply appear on the new page as "already dealt with".

  * There is no .env file in this zip. Your settings in cPanel stay
    as they are.
