HUNARSAAZ PORTAL — UPDATE
One email, sent only when you confirm the payment
=================================================

WHAT THIS CHANGES

1. NOBODY GETS AN EMAIL WHEN THEY REGISTER.
   The candidate fills in the form, attaches the payment proof, and is
   told on screen that their place is not confirmed yet and that we
   will write when the payment has been checked. No email at all.
   YOU get an email, so you know a registration is waiting.

2. ONE EMAIL, AND IT IS MS. ASMA AHMED'S LETTER.
   It goes out the moment you mark the registration confirmed, and
   nothing else sends it. It is her letter word for word, with the
   date, time, venue and speaker filled in from the workshop record.

3. CONFIRMING NOW WORKS FROM ANY OF THREE PLACES.
   Before, only one of them actually sent anything, which is why
   confirming in the admin appeared to do nothing. All three now do
   exactly the same thing:

     a) learn.hunarsaaz.pk/learn/workshops -> "Payment verified —
        approve"   (the easiest: the receipt is shown next to the
        amount, so you can check it there and then)
     b) /admin -> Workshop Registrations -> open the person ->
        Status -> Confirmed -> Save
     c) /admin -> Payments -> open the payment -> Status ->
        Confirmed -> Save

   Any of those sends the letter, marks the payment received, issues
   the receipt number, and records who confirmed it and when. Doing it
   twice does not send it twice.

4. REGISTRATION IS ONE STEP, NOT TWO.
   The form no longer says "Step 1 / Step 2 / Step 3". It is one
   registration, and it will not send at all until the payment proof
   is attached. The old separate "upload your receipt afterwards" page
   has been removed, so there is no way to be half-registered.

5. A WORKSHOPS TAB ON hunarsaaz.pk.
   New in the top menu, between Programs and Policy & Advisory. It
   opens a Workshops page listing everything that is on, with the
   date, the speaker, who it is for and a Register button.

   That page is served by the PORTAL, not by the static website. That
   is deliberate: the website is exported to plain HTML and uploaded,
   so a workshop added or closed in the admin would not show up there
   until somebody rebuilt and re-uploaded the whole site. Served by
   the portal, the list is simply correct whenever anyone looks, and
   you never have to touch the website again to add a workshop.

   THERE ARE THEREFORE TWO ZIPS THIS TIME. See "THE WEBSITE ZIP" at
   the end.

6. THE AUDITED ACCOUNTS ARE PUBLISHED.
   The audit report is on the website at

       https://hunarsaaz.pk/reports

   with a "Read the report" button that opens the PDF. It is also in
   the top menu: hover ABOUT and you will see three entries —
   About Us, Impact & Transparency, Reports & Accounts.

   Why under About rather than its own tab: a tenth item across the
   top does not fit a laptop screen, and it pushed Donate off the
   edge. Say the word if you would rather have it as its own tab and
   I will make room by moving something else.

   The old "our first annual report is on its way" paragraph on the
   Impact page is gone, replaced by the real document.

   Next year's report is one line of a file plus the PDF — tell me
   and I will add it.

7. THE FLYER.
   Sent separately. The Hunarsaaz logo is considerably larger, and
   "HR professionals and managers" now reads "Professionals". The
   wording on the website changes to match by itself.


WHAT YOU ARE UPLOADING

  The big node_modules folder has NOT changed. You do not touch it.
  Only these five:

      .next          (folder)
      public         (folder)
      src            (folder)
      server.js      (file)
      package.json   (file)


STEP BY STEP
============

STEP 1 — Unzip it on your computer

  Right-click  portal-update.zip  ->  "Extract All..."  ->  Extract.
  You get a folder  portal-update  holding the five things above.
  If you cannot see .next, in that window: View menu -> tick
  "Hidden items".


STEP 2 — Open FileZilla and connect

  On the RIGHT-hand side go into:   /hunarsaaz-portal
  If .next is not showing there: Server menu -> "Force showing hidden
  files".


STEP 3 — Rename the old .next (do not delete it this time)

  Right-click  .next  ->  Rename  ->  .next-old   ->  Enter.

  If there is no .next there at all, that is fine — carry on.


STEP 4 — Upload the five things

  On the LEFT, go into  portal-update.
  Select  .next, public, src, server.js, package.json
  Drag them to the RIGHT, into /hunarsaaz-portal.
  If asked: Action "Overwrite", tick "Always use this action", OK.

  WAIT until the queue at the bottom is completely empty.

  Then look at the bottom panel's "Failed transfers" tab. If anything
  is listed there, right-click -> "Reset and requeue all files" and
  let it finish. THIS MATTERS: one missing file out of two thousand
  is enough to make a page fail.


STEP 5 — Restart

  cPanel -> Setup Node.js App -> the portal app -> RESTART.
  Wait 30 seconds.


STEP 6 — Check

  1. https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/learn
  2. https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/workshops        (the new list)
  3. https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/workshops/thrive-360  (should be ONE
     form, no "Step 1", "Who can attend" says "Professionals")
  4. https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/learn/workshops  (after logging in)

  All four good -> Step 7.
  Anything black or broken -> see "IF A PAGE WILL NOT LOAD" below.


STEP 7 — Tidy up

  FileZilla -> right-click  .next-old  ->  Delete.


STEP 8 — Try it once, end to end

  Worth five minutes before the flyer goes out:

  1. Register yourself on the form with any photo as the receipt.
     You should get NO email.
  2. Confirm it at /learn/workshops.
  3. You should get exactly one email: the letter.

  If the letter looks right, you are ready.


IF A PAGE WILL NOT LOAD
=======================

  The portal now writes down exactly what it did to the database when
  it started. That line tells me what is wrong in one go:

    cPanel -> Setup Node.js App -> open the portal app. The screen
    shows the LOG FILE PATH. Open that file in File Manager (right-
    click -> View), go to the bottom, and look for lines beginning
    [schema].

  You will see either:

     [schema] database is up to date
       -> the database is fine, so it is a missing file. Go back to
          FileZilla and re-upload; check "Failed transfers".

  or:

     [schema] 2 statement(s) failed; ...
       -> send me those lines and I will fix it.

  To put the old version back at any time: delete the new .next,
  rename .next-old back to .next, RESTART.


NOTES
=====

  * Your database is not touched. Students, courses, payments and
    everyone who has already registered stay exactly as they are.
  * Anyone already marked confirmed will NOT be emailed again.
  * There is no .env file in this zip. Your cPanel settings stay as
    they are.
  * The Zoom link: /admin -> Workshops -> THRIVE 360 -> "Join URL".
    Until you set it, the letter says the platform will be shared by
    email on 10 November, which is what it should say.


THE WEBSITE ZIP  (hunarsaaz-website.zip)
========================================

  This is the second zip, and it is what puts the Workshops tab into
  the menu on hunarsaaz.pk. It is the whole marketing website as plain
  HTML — the same thing you have uploaded before.

  Do the portal first (steps 1 to 7 above), then this.

  1. Unzip  hunarsaaz-website.zip  on your computer.

  2. In FileZilla, on the RIGHT, go to the folder the website is
     served from — public_html (NOT hunarsaaz-portal).

  3. Drag EVERYTHING from inside the unzipped folder into it.
     Overwrite when asked, and tick "Always use this action".

     Note it contains a file called  .htaccess  — if FileZilla is not
     showing it on your side either, turn hidden files on, because
     clean URLs and the 404 page need it.

  4. Wait for the queue to empty, then open https://hunarsaaz.pk and
     check the menu. Workshops should be there, between Programs and
     Policy & Advisory, and clicking it should open the list on the
     portal.

  There is nothing to restart for the website — it is plain files.

  You only ever need to re-upload this zip if the WEBSITE changes.
  Adding or closing a workshop does not change the website.
