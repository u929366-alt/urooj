NOT public_html. The database in data/ holds every student account, grade

READ THIS FIRST — THE DATABASE IS REPLACED
------------------------------------------

This update adds new fields to courses, and a live site cannot add columns
to its own database. So data/hunarsaaz.db is REPLACED rather than kept.

The database in this zip contains:

    the three courses, published
    NO user accounts
    NO students, enrolments, payments or submissions

Replacing it therefore ERASES:

    your admin account (you create it again in step 6 — one minute)
    any course, student, enrolment, payment or submission on the site now

If you have real students, real payments or courses you typed in
yourself, STOP and say so before doing this. There is another way to do
it that keeps them; it is just slower.

If the only thing on the site is the admin account you made while
testing, carry on. That is the expected case.


STEP 1 — BACK UP WHAT IS THERE
------------------------------
cPanel > File Manager > /home2/lqonfyud/hunarsaaz-portal

Right-click each of these and Download, to your own computer:

    data/hunarsaaz.db
    public/uploads          (if it has anything in it)
    private-uploads         (if it has anything in it)

Keep them. If anything goes wrong they are how you get back.


STEP 2 — STOP THE APP
---------------------
cPanel > Setup Node.js App > your application > Stop

Replacing files under a running app can leave it half-updated.


STEP 3 — DELETE THE OLD APP FILES
---------------------------------
In /home2/lqonfyud/hunarsaaz-portal, delete these and only these:

    .next
    node_modules
    public
    src
    data
    server.js
    package.json
    START-HERE.txt

Leave private-uploads alone if it has student work in it.

Deleting .next and node_modules matters. Extracting over the top of them
leaves old files behind that can conflict with the new ones.


STEP 4 — UPLOAD AND EXTRACT
---------------------------
Upload hunarsaaz-portal.zip into /home2/lqonfyud/hunarsaaz-portal,
right-click it > Extract, then delete the zip.

Check afterwards that these sit DIRECTLY in hunarsaaz-portal, not inside
another folder:

    server.js   package.json   START-HERE-UPDATE.txt
    .next   node_modules   public   data   private-uploads   src

If they are one level down, go into that folder, Select All, Move them up
to /home2/lqonfyud/hunarsaaz-portal, and delete the empty folder.
Passenger looks for server.js at the top of the app root and nowhere else.

Turn on File Manager > Settings > Show Hidden Files to see .next.


STEP 5 — START IT
-----------------
Your environment variables are still set — cPanel keeps them, they are not
in the zip. Check they are all still there, then:

Setup Node.js App > Start (or Restart)

DO NOT click "Run NPM Install". Everything is installed already, and
running it can replace working Linux files with broken ones.

Open https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/learn/courses
You should see three courses: Digital Marketing, E-Commerce, Graphic Design.


STEP 6 — CREATE YOUR ADMIN ACCOUNT AGAIN
----------------------------------------
Go to https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk/admin

THE FIRST ACCOUNT MADE BECOMES THE ADMINISTRATOR. Do this immediately —
until you do, anyone who finds that page can claim it.

Use a real address such as ceo@hunarsaaz.pk and a strong password.


STEP 7 — ADD THE INSTRUCTORS
----------------------------
Each course shows a "Your instructor" section, and it only appears once a
course has one.

  1. /admin > Users > Create. Set Role to Instructor.
     Fill in Name, Headline (one line, e.g. "Master Trainer - 20 years in
     digital marketing"), Bio, and Avatar.
  2. /admin > Courses > open a course > Instructor > pick the person > Save.

The bio is what a student reads while deciding whether to enrol, so write
it for them rather than as a CV.

Only the name, headline, bio and photograph are shown publicly. The email,
phone and city on a user record are never put on the page.


STEP 8 — CHECK THE VIDEOS BEFORE YOU TELL ANYONE
------------------------------------------------
29 lessons have a video attached. Those links came from search results and
have NOT been played by us — the machine that built this cannot reach
YouTube.

Open each lesson with a video and check it plays and teaches that lesson.
Where one is wrong, replace it in /admin > Lessons > Video URL.

The other lessons have no video yet, deliberately. Their text stands on its
own.


WHAT IS IN THE COURSES
----------------------

  Digital Marketing   8 modules, 33 lessons, 8 quizzes, 8 assignments
  E-Commerce          5 modules, 17 lessons, 5 quizzes, 5 assignments

All three are marked "Aligned to the NAVTTC curriculum" on the course page.
That is your claim to stand behind: the syllabi were written to follow
NAVTTC's published trades, but nobody has yet compared them against the
actual curriculum documents. Get someone to do that.

None is marked NAVTTC-recognised, and none should be unless Hunarsaaz holds
that accreditation on paper. Students read it as what their certificate is
worth to an employer.

All three are free (price 0). Change a price in /admin > Courses if you
want to charge; a paid course holds the student's place until you confirm
their bank transfer at /learn/finance.
