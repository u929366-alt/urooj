hunarsaaz-website.zip — the main site at hunarsaaz.pk
=====================================================

WHAT THIS IS

The plain-HTML website: Home, About, Programs, Policy & Advisory,
Get Involved, Media, Contact. No Node.js, no database — it just sits in
public_html and is served as files.

This is NOT the learning portal. The portal is hunarsaaz-site.zip in this
same place, and it goes to learn.hunarsaaz.pk. Do not mix the two up:
this one belongs in public_html, that one must never go near it.

WHAT CHANGED

Asma Ahmed added to the Policy & Advisory page, alongside the other
senior advisers and associates.


HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE

1. Download hunarsaaz-website.zip to your computer.

2. cPanel > File Manager > public_html

3. BACK UP FIRST. Select everything currently in public_html, click
   Compress, and save the resulting zip somewhere outside public_html —
   your home directory is fine. If anything goes wrong you can put the old
   site back from it.

4. Delete the old site files from public_html.

   Leave these alone if you see them — they are not part of the website:
     .well-known      cgi-bin       any folder named after a subdomain
     (learn.hunarsaaz.pk lives next to public_html, not inside it, so it
     is not affected either way)

5. Upload hunarsaaz-website.zip into public_html.

6. Right-click it > Extract, into public_html.

7. Delete hunarsaaz-website.zip.

8. Check that .htaccess is there. In File Manager, Settings (top right) >
   tick "Show Hidden Files (dotfiles)". Without .htaccess the addresses
   with no .html on the end stop working.

9. Open https://hunarsaaz.pk/about/ and press Ctrl+Shift+R. Asma Ahmed
   should appear under Advisers.


IF THE PAGES LOOK UNSTYLED

You have a half-uploaded site: the HTML arrived but the _next folder did
not. Extract the zip again over the top.


NEXT TIME

Every text or photo change on these pages means a fresh zip from us — the
pages are built files, not editable in an admin panel. The learning
portal is the opposite: courses, lessons and students are all managed at
learn.hunarsaaz.pk/admin without any upload.
