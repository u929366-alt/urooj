HUNARSAAZ WEBSITE + LEARNING PORTAL — READY TO UPLOAD
=====================================================

This folder is the finished website, already built. Nothing needs to be
compiled on the server. It was built on Linux, which is what your hosting
runs, so the compiled parts are the correct ones.


STEP 1 — MAKE AN EMAIL ADDRESS FOR THE SITE
-------------------------------------------
Do this first, because you need its details in step 4.

  cPanel > Email Accounts > Create
  Suggested address:  noreply@hunarsaaz.pk

Then open that account's "Connect Devices" page and note the outgoing
(SMTP) server and port. It is usually mail.hunarsaaz.pk on port 465, and
the username is the full email address.

This sends password resets, payment instructions and receipts. Without
it, people who forget their password cannot get back in, and donors get
no reference to quote on their transfer.


STEP 2 — CREATE THE NODE.JS APP (this makes the folder for you)
---------------------------------------------------------------
  cPanel > Setup Node.js App > Create Application

    Node.js version           20 or higher
    Application mode          Production
    Application root          hunarsaaz-portal
    Application URL           learn.hunarsaaz.pk   (use a subdomain first,
                              so the live site cannot break; switch to
                              hunarsaaz.pk once you are happy)
    Application startup file  server.js

"Application root" is a folder name. cPanel creates it inside your home
directory, so you end up with:

    /home/YOUR-CPANEL-USERNAME/hunarsaaz-portal

Your cPanel username is shown in the sidebar of cPanel.

DO NOT put it in public_html, or anywhere inside public_html.

Everything under public_html can be downloaded by anyone who knows the
address. The database in data/hunarsaaz.db holds every student account,
grade and payment record — inside public_html, a stranger could simply
download the whole thing. The application folder must sit NEXT TO
public_html, not inside it. cPanel joins the two together for you; that
is what the Application URL field does.


STEP 3 — UPLOAD
---------------
In cPanel > File Manager, open the folder from step 2:

    /home/YOUR-CPANEL-USERNAME/hunarsaaz-portal

Upload everything from this zip into it, and keep the folder structure
exactly as it is. You should end up with server.js, package.json,
START-HERE.txt and the folders .next, node_modules, public, data and
private-uploads all sitting directly inside hunarsaaz-portal.

Tip: uploading the zip itself and using File Manager's Extract is far
quicker than uploading thousands of individual files.


STEP 4 — ADD THE ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES
--------------------------------------
Go back to cPanel > Setup Node.js App, open the application you made in
step 2, and add these in its Environment Variables section:

  PAYLOAD_SECRET          a long random string of your own
  NODE_ENV                production
  SITE_URL                https://learn.hunarsaaz.pk
                          (change it when you move to the main domain)

  SMTP_HOST               mail.hunarsaaz.pk        from step 1
  SMTP_PORT               465
  SMTP_USER               noreply@hunarsaaz.pk     from step 1
  SMTP_PASS               that mailbox's password
  EMAIL_FROM_ADDRESS      noreply@hunarsaaz.pk
  EMAIL_FROM_NAME         Hunarsaaz

PAYLOAD_SECRET signs the login cookies. Make it long and random, keep it
private, and do not change it later or everyone will be signed out.
Leave DATABASE_URI unset.

DO NOT click "Run NPM Install". Everything is already installed, and
running it can replace the Linux files with broken ones.


STEP 5 — CHECK FOLDER PERMISSIONS (755 is fine)
-----------------------------------------------
These three are written to while the site runs:

  data/                  the database
  public/uploads/        course images and lesson files
  private-uploads/       student assignment submissions


STEP 6 — START IT
-----------------
Click Restart, then open your site.


STEP 7 — CREATE YOUR ACCOUNT
----------------------------
Go to /admin and fill in the form. THE FIRST ACCOUNT AUTOMATICALLY
BECOMES THE ADMIN. Use a real address such as ceo@hunarsaaz.pk and a
strong password.

Then add your courses: Courses first, then Modules inside a course, then
Lessons inside a module. Leave a course's Price at 0 to keep it free.


STEP 8 — TEST IT
----------------
Two things worth checking straight away:

  Password reset  /learn/login > "Forgotten your password?" > your own
                  address. The email should arrive within a minute. If it
                  does not, the SMTP details in step 4 are wrong.

  Donation        /donate > fill it in. You should land on a page showing
                  your bank details and a reference, and get an email.
                  The donation then appears at /learn/finance for you to
                  confirm once the money arrives.


WHERE THINGS ARE
----------------

  /                     the public website
  /donate               donations
  /learn/courses        course catalogue, open to everyone
  /learn                a student's own courses and progress
  /learn/finance        STAFF: confirm payments that have arrived
  /learn/teach          STAFF: grading and student progress
  /admin                STAFF: courses, lessons, users, everything else


ABOUT PAYMENTS
--------------
There is no card gateway. Stripe and PayPal do not accept Pakistani
organisations, and you have no local gateway account yet, so money comes
by bank transfer:

  1. Donor or student is given a REFERENCE and your Allied Bank details.
  2. They transfer the money quoting that reference.
  3. You open /learn/finance and click "Mark received".
  4. A receipt is emailed automatically. If it was a course fee, the
     course opens for that student at the same moment.

A student who has not paid gets NOTHING — no lessons, quizzes,
assignments or discussion. Their place is simply held.

Check the amount on the bank statement matches before confirming. The
site cannot verify that for you.


BACKUPS — PLEASE READ
---------------------

data/hunarsaaz.db holds EVERY student account, grade, certificate and
payment record, and it is the only copy. Download it regularly.

When you upload a newer version of the site, never overwrite these three:

  data/            public/uploads/            private-uploads/

Doing so erases student records, their submitted work, and your payment
history.
